🔀 Board Differences

Board specifications differ in assessment structure and emphasis. The core content is shared across boards; these are the genuine differences by board:

AQA: Two papers: Government of the UK (constitution, Parliament, PM and cabinet, judiciary) and Politics of the USA plus political ideas. 30-mark essays throughout; no coursework.
Edexcel: Three papers: UK Politics with core political ideas; UK Government with non-core political ideas; and either USA or global politics. 30-mark essays; no coursework.
