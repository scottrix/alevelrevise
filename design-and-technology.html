🔧 A-Level Design and Technology

Foundation Higher All Boards

Complete revision notes for A-Level Design and Technology covering 24 topics across 4 strands. Aligned with the AQA 8552 specification. Covers Core Technical Principles, Materials and Their Working Properties, Specialist Technical Principles, and Designing and Making Principles — including new technologies, material properties, manufacturing processes, NEA coursework guidance and exam technique.

📊 Course Overview

A-Level Design and Technology (AQA 8552) is assessed through a written exam (50%) and a Non-Exam Assessment (NEA) coursework project (50%). The written exam has three sections: Section A covers Core Technical Principles (20 marks), Section B covers Specialist Technical Principles (30 marks), and Section C covers Designing and Making Principles (50 marks). The NEA involves a design-and-make task from a contextual challenge set by AQA.
