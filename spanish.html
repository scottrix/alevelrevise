🔀 Board Differences

Board specifications differ in assessment structure and emphasis. The core content is shared across boards; these are the genuine differences by board:

AQA: Paper 1: listening, reading and writing (translation both ways). Paper 2: two essays on set literature or film. Paper 3: speaking with the Individual Research Project.
Edexcel: Paper 1: listening, reading and translation. Paper 2: written response to works and translation. Paper 3: speaking on a theme and the IRP.
WJEC/Eduqas: Oral unit (IRP), listening and reading unit, and critical response essays on set works.
CCEA: Northern Ireland's board: speaking, listening and reading units plus a written paper with set-work essays.
