🎭 A-Level Drama

Foundation Higher All Boards

Complete revision notes for A-Level Drama covering 26 topics across 6 strands. Aligned with the AQA 8261 specification. Covers Theatrical Terms and Conventions, Practitioners and Styles, Devising Drama, Performing Texts, Live Theatre Review, and Design and Technical Theatre — including practitioner theory, performance skills, portfolio writing and exam technique.

📊 Course Overview

A-Level Drama is assessed through three components: a written exam (Understanding Drama, 40%) and two NEA components (Devising Drama 30%, Texts in Practice 30%). Students devise original drama, perform extracts from scripts, and analyse live theatre. The written exam tests knowledge of a set text and evaluation of live theatre.

ComponentDescriptionWeighting
Understanding DramaWritten exam: Section A (set text), Section B (live theatre review)40%
Devising DramaNEA: Devised performance + devising portfolio30%
Texts in PracticeNEA: Two performed extracts from a play (visiting examiner)30%

🎤 Key Practitioners

Stanislavski: Naturalism, system, given circumstances, magic if, emotion memory

Brecht: Epic theatre, alienation effect, gestus, political theatre

Artaud: Theatre of Cruelty, total theatre, assault on the senses

Frantic Assembly: Physical theatre, chair work, push hands, lift and lean

Boal: Theatre of the Oppressed, forum theatre, spect-actors
