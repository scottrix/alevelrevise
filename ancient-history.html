Board specifications differ in assessment structure and emphasis. The core content is shared across boards; these are the genuine differences by board:

OCR: Two written papers: Greece (period study plus in-depth options on Sparta, Athens or Alexander) and Rome (period study plus the Julio-Claudians or Britain). Essay and source-based analysis.
