Board-Specific Note

WJEC specification; mainly taken in Wales.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain DNA structure and replication
  • Use Punnett squares
  • Describe gene expression

Key Points

  • DNA double helix, base pairing
  • Alleles, dominant and recessive
  • Punnett squares predict ratios

Example Question

For a cross of two heterozygotes (Aa x Aa), phenotypic ratio?

Model Answer

3:1 dominant to recessive
