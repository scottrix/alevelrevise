Physics — Electricity and Circuits

Exam board: Edexcel  |  Back to subjects

Board-Specific Note

Edexcel (Pearson) specification; check the specific content list for your paper.

Learning Objectives

  • Apply Ohm's law
  • Combine resistors in series and parallel
  • Work with power and energy

Key Points

  • V = IR
  • Series R = R₁+R₂, parallel 1/R = 1/R₁+1/R₂
  • P = IV = I²R

Example Question

Current through a 6 V, 12 Ω lamp?

Model Answer

I = 6/12 = 0.5 A

Practice Questions

  • Two 4 Ω resistors in series, total resistance?
  • Power of a 2 A device at 12 V
  • Voltage across a 3 Ω resistor carrying 2 A
