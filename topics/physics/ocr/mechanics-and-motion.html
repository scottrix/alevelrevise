Key Points

  • v = u + at, s = ut + ½at²
  • F = ma
  • p = mv, KE = ½mv²
  • Conservation of momentum

Example Question

A car accelerates from rest at 2 m/s² for 5 s. Distance?

Model Answer

s = ½(2)(25) = 25 m
