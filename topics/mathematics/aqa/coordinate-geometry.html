Mathematics — Coordinate Geometry

Board-Specific Note

Spec taught as Pure, Mechanics, and Statistics papers.

Learning Objectives

  • Find the equation of a line
  • Compute distance and midpoint
  • Handle parallel and perpendicular lines

Key Points

  • Gradient m = (y₂−y₁)/(x₂−x₁)
  • Line y = mx + c
  • Distance √((x₂−x₁)²+(y₂−y₁)²)
  • Midpoint ((x₁+x₂)/2,(y₁+y₂)/2)

Example Question

Find the equation of the line through (1,2) and (3,6)

Model Answer

y = 2x

Practice Questions

  • Find line through (0,0) and (2,5)
  • Are y=2x+1 and y=2x−3 parallel?
  • Find intersection of y=x+1 and y=−x+3
