Further Mathematics — Complex Numbers

Exam board: OCR  |  Back to subjects

Board-Specific Note

Frequently paired with the 'MEI' specification; strong applied component.

Learning Objectives

  • Work with complex arithmetic
  • Represent numbers on the Argand diagram
  • Solve polynomial equations with complex roots

Key Points

  • z = a + bi
  • Conjugate z̄ = a − bi
  • Argand diagram and modulus/argument
  • Roots of quadratics come in conjugate pairs

Example Question

Solve z² + 4 = 0

Model Answer

z = ±2i

Practice Questions

  • Add 3+4i and 5−2i
  • Multiply (2+i)(3−i)
  • Solve z² + 2z + 5 = 0
