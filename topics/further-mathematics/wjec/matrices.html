Further Mathematics — Matrices

Exam board: WJEC  |  Back to subjects

Board-Specific Note

Pure plus applied options; check the specific pathway chosen.

Learning Objectives

  • Add and multiply matrices
  • Compute determinants and inverses
  • Solve systems with matrices

Key Points

  • Matrix multiplication is non-commutative
  • det(A) for 2x2 = ad−bc
  • A⁻¹ = (1/det) adj(A)

Example Question

Find determinant of [[2,3],[1,4]]

Model Answer

8 − 3 = 5

Practice Questions

  • Multiply [[1,2],[3,4]] by [[5],[6]]
  • Find the inverse of [[2,0],[0,3]]
  • Solve 2x+3y=5, y=1 using matrices
