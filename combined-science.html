🧬 A-Level Combined Science (Trilogy)

Foundation Higher All Boards

Complete revision notes for A-Level Combined Science Trilogy covering all 77 topics across Biology, Chemistry and Physics. Aligned with AQA, Edexcel, OCR and CCEA specifications. Double award qualification.

📊 Course Overview

A-Level Combined Science Trilogy covers Biology, Chemistry and Physics. Students take 6 papers (2 per science) and receive a double grade (e.g. 6-6, 7-7). Foundation tier covers grades 1-1 to 5-5; Higher tier covers grades 4-4 to 9-9.

🧬 Biology (22 Topics)

Cell biology, organisation, infection, bioenergetics, homeostasis, inheritance, variation, evolution and ecology.

🧪 Chemistry (26 Topics)

Atomic structure, bonding, quantitative chemistry, chemical changes, energy changes, rates, organic chemistry, analysis, atmosphere and resources.

⚙️ Physics (28 Topics)

Energy, electricity, particle model, atomic structure, forces, waves and electromagnetism.
