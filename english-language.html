🔀 Board Differences

Board specifications differ in assessment structure and emphasis. The core content is shared across boards; these are the genuine differences by board:

AQA: Paper 1: language, the individual and society (textual variations, child language acquisition). Paper 2: language diversity and change. NEA: language investigation plus original writing (20% of A-level).
OCR: Paper 1: exploring language (topics such as language and gender, language and journalism). Paper 2: dimensions of linguistic variation and child language. NEA: independent language investigation (20%).
WJEC/Eduqas: Component 1: language and identity with a spoken language study; Component 2: language change over time plus a directed writing task; NEA: language investigation (20%).
