🔀 Board Differences

Board specifications differ in assessment structure and emphasis. The core content is shared across boards; these are the genuine differences by board:

OCR: Two written papers on media language, representation, industries and audiences with close study products (70%), plus a media production NEA with statement of intent (30%).
WJEC/Eduqas: Component 1 and 2 written exams on all four framework frameworks and set products (70%); cross-media production NEA (30%).
