🔀 Board Differences

Board specifications differ in assessment structure and emphasis. The core content is shared across boards; these are the genuine differences by board:

AQA: Three papers: the English legal system and criminal law; tort; and further law (contract or human rights). Problem questions and essays; no coursework.
OCR: Three components: the legal system and criminal law; law making and the law of tort; the nature of law and either contract or human rights. Scenario-based problem questions.
WJEC/Eduqas: Unitised: the English legal system and law making; criminal law and tort; plus broader legal concepts and evaluation essays.
