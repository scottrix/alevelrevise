🔀 Board Differences

Board specifications differ in assessment structure and emphasis. The core content is shared across boards; these are the genuine differences by board:

AQA: Three papers: education with methods in context; topics in sociology (families, beliefs, media); crime and deviance with theory and methods. No coursework, 80 marks per paper.
OCR: Three papers: socialisation, culture and identity; researching social inequalities; debating and researching social issues (globalisation, crime). Includes a compulsory research methods section.
WJEC/Eduqas: Unitised: contemporary families and households; education and research methods; crime, deviance and social stratification.
